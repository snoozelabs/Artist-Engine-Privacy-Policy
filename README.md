## 2. Information We Collect

### 2(a) Google user data accessed via OAuth

The Branding Blueprint does not use Google as the primary sign-in method for Artist Engine. Account login is performed with a passwordless email magic link through Firebase Authentication.

Separately, if you choose to connect Google Calendar in Artist Engine Settings (or during optional tool setup), we request the following Google OAuth scopes and process only the data needed for the features described in Section 3:

- **https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar** — allows Artist Engine to read and write events on your primary Google Calendar so we can create calendar events from plans you generate in the App (for example, titles, descriptions, start/end times, optional location, and reminder settings).

- **https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly** — allows Artist Engine to read events from your primary Google Calendar so we can sync relevant event details into your in-app calendar view.

- **https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email** — allows us to obtain the Google account email address associated with the connected Calendar integration so we can display which Google account is connected and store that association securely with your Artist Engine account.

- **https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile** — allows access to basic Google profile information associated with the OAuth grant. In practice, we use this connection primarily to identify the linked Google account (including email) for the Calendar integration.

When Calendar is connected, we may store Google OAuth access and refresh tokens, the connected Google email address, and synced calendar event fields such as event identifiers, titles/summaries, start and end times, duration, and location. These are stored under your user record in our database for the integration to function.

We do not request or use Google Drive, Gmail, YouTube, Google Sheets, Google Contacts, or Google Photos scopes.

### 2(b) Information you provide directly

Depending on how you use Artist Engine, you may provide:

- Email address used to sign in;
- Display name and profile photo (URL or uploaded image);
- Onboarding and questionnaire answers (for example, artist role, genres, location, goals, brand vibe, audience, content style);
- Brand identity information (content pillars, voice profile, visual identity notes);
- Creative work product such as ideas, notes, tags, attachments/text references, studio projects and tasks;
- In-app calendar commitments and plans you create;
- Messages and media you post in Community features (where available to your account);
- Co-Pilot / Strategist chat prompts and related brand or project context you choose to send;
- Optional Notion workspace connection details if you connect Notion;
- Subscription and billing-related choices (processed via Stripe).

### 2(c) Automatically collected information

We and our infrastructure providers may automatically collect:

- Firebase Authentication identifiers (user ID) and authentication metadata;
- Device push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) if notifications are enabled;
- App diagnostics and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics);
- Limited analytics events (Firebase Analytics), such as app opens and feature usage signals;
- Server logs generated by Cloud Functions when you use features such as OAuth callbacks, billing webhooks, or Co-Pilot requests.

Our Firebase Hosting pages (including this Privacy Policy and sign-in helper pages) are static documents. We do not run advertising cookies on those pages. Browser or device storage used by Firebase Authentication and related Google/Firebase SDKs may still be required for secure sign-in and App operation.

## 3. How We Use Google User Data

Google user data obtained through the scopes above is used only to provide and improve Calendar-related features you enable:

- **Calendar read scope / Calendar API read:** Sync events from your primary Google Calendar into Artist Engine so you can see commitments alongside your Artist Engine plans.
- **Calendar write scope / Calendar API create:** Create events on your primary Google Calendar when you choose actions in Artist Engine that send a plan or event to Google Calendar.
- **userinfo.email / userinfo.profile:** Identify and display the Google account connected to Calendar integration and associate tokens with the correct Artist Engine user.

We do not use Google Calendar or Google profile data for advertising, resale, unrelated profiling, or training generalized AI/ML models.

Connecting Google Calendar is optional; you can use many Artist Engine features without connecting Google.

## 4. How We Use Other Information

We use non-Google information to:

- Create and secure your account and authenticate you;
- Personalize brand, content, learning, and planning features;
- Operate Co-Pilot / Strategist responses based on prompts and context you provide;
- Provide community, notifications, subscriptions, and customer support;
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, debug errors, and understand product usage at an aggregate/operational level;
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information.  
We do not sell Google user data.

We share information only with service providers that help us operate Artist Engine, under contractual and technical controls appropriate to the service:

- **Google Firebase / Google Cloud** — authentication, database (Cloud Firestore), file storage (Cloud Storage), Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics, and push messaging.
- **Stripe** — payment processing and customer portal for subscriptions. Stripe processes payment details according to its own privacy policy; we store subscription/customer identifiers needed to manage access.
- **OpenAI** — when you use Co-Pilot / Strategist features, relevant prompts and context you provide (such as brand profile or idea/project context) are sent via our secure Cloud Functions to generate responses. We do not send Google OAuth tokens to OpenAI.
- **Notion** — only if you voluntarily connect Notion, so we can sync pages/databases you authorize into App features such as ideas or pillars.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer (for example, a merger), in which case we will continue to protect personal information in accordance with this Policy or provide notice of changes.

## 8. Data Storage and Security

Artist Engine application data is stored primarily on Google Firebase / Google Cloud infrastructure (including Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) associated with our production project.

Google OAuth tokens for Calendar are stored in your private integration record in Firestore and are used by our backend/client services only to perform the Calendar actions you request.

We use industry-standard protections provided by these platforms, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for App and API communications, and encryption at rest as implemented by Google Cloud / Firebase for stored data. Access to production systems is limited to authorized personnel with a need to operate or support the service.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account and App data for as long as your account remains active and as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal/accounting requirements.

Google Calendar integration data is retained while the integration remains connected. If you disconnect Google Calendar in Artist Engine, we delete the stored Google Calendar integration record (including stored OAuth tokens) from your account integrations.

Synced calendar event copies previously written into your App calendar data may remain as App content unless you delete those items or request account deletion. You can remove individual ideas, projects, and similar content within the App where those controls are available.

**How to request deletion of your Artist Engine account and personal data:**

Email artistonboarding@thebrandingblueprint.com.au from the email address associated with your account with the subject:

**"Delete my Artist Engine account"**

We will verify your request and delete or anonymize personal data we control, except where retention is required by law (for example, limited billing records).

The App also includes a "Request deletion" entry point under Settings → Your data; you may use that flow when enabled, or contact us directly as described above.

## 10. Revoking Access

You may disconnect Google Calendar at any time in Artist Engine (Settings / Connected Tools), which removes our stored Calendar integration credentials.

You can also revoke The Branding Blueprint's access to your Google Account at any time in Google's permissions settings:

https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

After revocation, Calendar sync/create features that depend on Google access will stop working until you reconnect and grant permission again.
